Intervenable Audio Scene Graphs: audio examples

Each example gives the original recording, the edit in words, our result, and the ground truth (the edited scene rendered in closed form by the same physical renderer that made the original). All clips are 4 s at 16 kHz. Spectrograms are mel scale.

How the examples were chosen: a scene must first pass fixed audibility rules (every named source is within 15 dB of the loudest and active for at least 40% of the clip; room edits must change the level in the gaps by more than 3 dB). Second, the edit must matter and our result must be a real edit: our output must be at least 4 dB closer to the ground truth (SI-SDR) than the unedited mixture, and at least 5 dB from the mixture itself. Among the scenes that pass, the three shown per edit are the ones with the largest gain over the unedited mixture. They are therefore favourable examples, not typical ones. The medians in the paper and the listening-test trials come from the full cohort and from the audibility rules alone. An edit with no passing scene says so.

Code, result files and the listening-test package: code/ (this repository). Model checkpoints are attached to the GitHub release of the same repository.
